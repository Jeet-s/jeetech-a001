Envelope Mini: Envelope Budget

Envelope budget app & simple budget planner: private and offline.

Get it on Google Play

An envelope budget app helps you see what you have allocated, spent and have remaining in each spending category. Enter your own numbers, organize everyday expenses and review your budget without connecting a bank account.

Looking for a budget planner free app with flexible categories? Start with sample envelopes or create your own, then record your first expense. No account or sign-in is needed.

SEE YOUR BUDGET CLEARLY
• Create envelopes and subcategories with icons and colors.
• View allocated, spent and remaining amounts together.
• Choose rollover for each envelope and see positive or negative amounts carried into the next period.
• Use monthly periods or a paycheck cycle that matches how you budget.

MAKE MANUAL ENTRY EASIER
Use this envelope budget app to record purchases as they happen or review them later. As a manual budget tracker, it provides practical shortcuts while leaving you in control of each entry.
• Add expenses and income with an inline calculator and saved defaults.
• Duplicate a transaction or split a purchase across envelopes.
• Organize entries with labels and search filters.
• Track separate account balances manually. Record transfers between your tracked accounts without counting them as income or spending; the app does not move money.

REVIEW YOUR ROUTINE
• Set up recurring expenses and income with flexible schedules.
• Review cash flow and category charts for selected date ranges.
• Use reminders to return to logging and period reviews.
• Import and export CSV files for your own records.

FREE FEATURES
For people comparing budget planner free app options, the free tier includes core envelope budgeting, rollover, recurring entries, charts, CSV import/export, labels, split and duplicate tools, security lock, and base and dark themes. The free version shows ads.

WHAT PRO ADDS
The one-time Pro unlock adds advanced reports, bulk editing and additional themes, and removes ads. A separate Remove Ads purchase is also available. Review the purchase screen for pricing and included features.

YOUR ENTRIES AND DATA
Your budget entries, notes and settings stay on your device and are not sent to our servers. You can delete those entries in Settings → Delete all data. There is no cloud sync or cloud backup.

The app sends anonymous, minimal usage analytics to improve the app. With notification permission, it registers a device push token and random install ID with our server for reminders and updates. The ad provider collects and shares advertising, approximate location, interaction and diagnostic data as described in the privacy policy.

This is a personal budgeting tool, not financial advice.
